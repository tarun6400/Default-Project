The Problem

Every extra hand in the chain takes a cut

A farmer sells paddy at ₹1,450 per quintal. The same rice reaches a household at ₹2,900. The difference did not reach any farmer — it was absorbed across the chain.

Retail price paid by consumer
₹2,900

₹1,500
What the farmer actually receives
₹1,450

Illustrative paddy supply chain, per quintal. ~50% of the shelf price never reaches the farmer.

Farmers earn less

With no direct line to buyers, farmers have no bargaining power and rarely see the final market price. They accept whatever is offered at the nearest mandi.

Consumers pay more

Each layer adds logistics cost, wastage and margin. The same commodity is priced several times over before it reaches the shelf, and small vendors feel it hardest.

Chains get longer

Storage, repacking, unsold stock and delayed payment compound the cost. Produce spoils while it sits across 3–4 storage points before anyone buys it.

No price visibility

Farmers have no access to market intelligence, so they cannot plan planting, time their harvest or negotiate from a position of knowledge.

The Solution

One platform. Three layers. Zero middlemen.

FarmDirect replaces the chain of traders with a single transparent digital marketplace backed by forecasting and logistics intelligence.

01

Direct Marketplace

Farmers and FPOs list produce publicly with a visible asking price. Buyers — households, kirana stores, hotels, hostels, caterers, exporters — order directly. No agents, no commission, no undisclosed deductions.

  • Public price listing with grade and quantity
  • Verified FPO and farmer profiles
  • Direct chat and order negotiation
  • Digital receipts and payment trail

02

Demand Prediction Engine

A forecasting model trained on mandi price history, seasonal patterns, weather, festival calendars and local event data. It tells farmers what will be in demand, where, and roughly when — so they harvest and sell into a market that actually exists.

  • 7 / 14 / 30-day demand forecast per crop and district
  • Price band prediction with confidence score
  • Over-supply and shortage early warnings
  • Harvest-window recommendations by region
03

Smart Logistics & Consolidation

Small lots from neighbouring farms are pooled into shared, full-value vehicle loads. Routing is optimised by proximity and load factor, cutting per-kilometre cost for every farm in the pool and shrinking the delivery window.

  • Automatic lot aggregation from nearby farms
  • Route optimisation with live tracking
  • Cold-chain and quality-checked handling
  • Split delivery to multiple small buyers

How It Works

From the field to your door in five steps

  1. 1
    🌱

    List

    The farmer or FPO uploads crop, grade, quantity and an asking price. Verification happens through geo-tagged harvest proof and FPO records.

  2. 2
    🔮

    Forecast

    Demand intelligence matches the listing to likely buyers in the region and suggests a competitive price band before the produce moves.

  3. 3
    🤝

    Match & Confirm

    Buyers receive matched offers and place orders directly with the farm. Contracts, weights and grades are recorded on-platform — both sides see the same terms.

  4. 4
    🚚

    Consolidate & Ship

    Nearby farm lots are pooled into one vehicle load. The shipment is tracked door to door, with quality checks at collection.

  5. 5
    💸

    Settle

    Payment is released on confirmed delivery, straight to the farmer's account or FPO pool. Receipts, weight slips and deductions — if any — are itemised in the app.

Technology

Built for the realities of Indian farming

📡

Low-bandwidth first

Progressive loading, offline listing drafts over SMS/USSD fallback, and image compression on upload so it works on a 2G connection in a village.

🗣️

Voice and regional language

Full UI in English, हिन्दी, తెలుగు, বাংলা, मराठी, தமிழ் and more. Voice-assisted listing for low-literacy users — speak the crop and quantity, the form fills itself.

🤖

Forecast & price intelligence

Gradient-boosted regression over mandi history, rainfall, festival and event signals, with a confidence band so farmers know how much to trust the number.

🧾

Verified, tamper-proof records

Geo-tagged harvest proof, digital weighbridge slips and a full price trail. Every transaction leaves an auditable record that regulators can inspect.

🚜

Route optimisation

Consolidation engine clusters nearby lots, fills vehicles to a target load factor and splits deliveries across buyers — cutting cost per kilogram for small farms.

🛡️

Direct government rails

Built to interoperate with e-NAM, NAFED, FPO registries and state MSP procurement, with transparent grievance and dispute escalation.
